# Third-Party Notices

## Cantarell

`Fonts/Cantarell-VF.otf` is the variable Cantarell font, version 0.301, distributed unmodified as Fedora packages it in `abattis-cantarell-vf-fonts`. The tests use it for its weight axis from 100 to 800 and its proportional figures with a `tnum` feature.
